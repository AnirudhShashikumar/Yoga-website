## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of editorial luxury and disciplined traditional practice. Built for a certified master practitioner (M.Sc in Yoga, NIS Certified), the visual tone avoids commercialized spiritual clichés—eliminating decorative mandalas, artificial neon auras, and ornamental lotus motifs—in favor of architectural balance, expansive breath-like whitespace, and grounded editorial typography.

The design philosophy aligns with calm luxury (akin to modern wellness leaders like Alo Yoga, Apple Health, and Kinfolk), utilizing tactile warmth, quiet authority, and functional clarity. The interface must evoke:
- **Steadiness (Sthira):** Deep forest pine tones, disciplined grid structures, and sharp informational hierarchy.
- **Ease (Sukha):** Generous padding, organic card radii, delicate borders, and soothing unhurried transitions.
- **Authenticity:** Prioritization of natural daylight photography, unpretentious linework, and clear pedagogical credentials over trendy wellness aesthetics.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from natural ashram environments—raw limestone, morning pine needles, soft sage leaves, and warm brass instruments.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#1E3A2F`):** Deep Forest Green. Used for primary interactive actions, high-contrast display titles, focused states, and foundational brand emphasis. Communicates grounded authority and intellectual depth.
- **Secondary (`#C8E0D1`):** Soft Morning Sage. Serves as gentle surface fills, badge backgrounds, pill highlights, and subtle tone breaks against neutral backdrops.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059`):** Sand Ochre. Applied sparingly to signify master credentials, special workshop tags, subtle accents, and secondary focus indicators.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm Ivory Linen. Acts as the universal canvas background, preventing optical glare and offering a softer, more organic reading surface than pure digital white.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for elevated component cards, interactive sheets, and floating modals to ensure distinct layer separation.

### Functional Tint Accents
- **Serene Sky (`#DCEAF2` / `#6C96A8`):** Muted cool accent for pranayama, breathwork modules, and informational states requiring quiet neutrality.
- **Subtle Outline (`rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08)`):** A balanced organic border hue derived from the primary green, avoiding lifeless gray strokes.

## Typography

Typography pairs the refined, classical elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clear, humanist geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Headlines use Playfair Display in moderate weights (500 to 600). Titles are typeset with disciplined line heights and tight tracking to project editorial curation without sacrificing legibility for older demographics.
- **Body & Information Delivery:** Plus Jakarta Sans is deployed across all running body text, schedule timetables, pricing tables, and UI controls. Its generous x-height and open counters maintain effortless scanning, supporting all age groups seeking mindful movement or therapeutic consultation.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Badges, category markers, and session durations use uppercase or wide-tracked title case in `label-sm` and `label-md` to establish quiet, clear wayfinding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, rhythmic distribution of negative space that allows the user's mind to decompress while navigating:
- **Canvas Framework:** A centered 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum content width capped at `1280px`. Mobile viewports compress to a 4-column structure with `1.25rem` outer gutters.
- **Spacious Vertical Rhythm:** Section separations should be wide and unhurried—utilizing `space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` to `7rem` between major thematic domains (e.g., Course Overview to Teacher Lineage).
- **Component Breathing Room:** Cards and modular sheets favor internal paddings of `1.5rem` to `2rem` (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to preserve clean boundary margins between body typography and edge borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established organically through warm-tinted daylight ambient shadows and delicate surface tonal layers, avoiding synthetic digital drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Neutral Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`).
  - **Raised Modules:** Pure Linen White (`#FFFFFF`) with a hair-thin perimeter stroke of `rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08)`.
  - **Nested Insets:** Light Sage or Air Sky tint (`#DDECE3` or `#DCEAF2` at 40% opacity) for registration blocks, quotes, or schedule timeslots.
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - **Resting Cards:** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.04)`.
  - **Floating Controls & Modals:** `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.09)`.
  - **WhatsApp & Sticky Actions:** A subtle, warm-tinted lift: `0 8px 28px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system balances modern structural cleanliness with soothing, organic contours:
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`):** Workshop cards, instructor profile units, retreat modules, and floating modals adopt an expansive 24px (`1.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Interactive Triggers (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Inputs, select menus, and secondary modules feature a comfortable 16px (`1rem`) border radius.
- **Pill Enclosures (`9999px`):** CTAs, status chips, teacher certification tags, and the floating WhatsApp trigger use full pill geometry for tactile, friendly ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Forest Green (`#1E3A2F`) fill, crisp white text, full pill-shaped (`9999px`) border radius. Height: 52px for broad touch accessibility. Transition: soft transform hover with slight color lightening (`#233D33`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Warm Ivory fill, 1px solid stroke (`rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.2)`), Deep Forest Green text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure transparent background, underlined editorial text in Sand Ochre (`#C5A059`) or Deep Forest Green with an understated arrow glyph.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), 24px border radius (`rounded-xl`), 1px outline in `rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08)`, and diffuse ambient shadow.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratios favor 4:5 or 16:10 editorial formats with rounded inner corners matching card boundaries (16px inner radius). Photography should highlight authentic poses, studio natural light, and natural textures.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** 52px height, 16px radius, neutral off-white surface (`#F5F2EB`), and subtle border. Focus states highlight with a 1.5px Deep Forest Green outline and zero harsh drop shadow.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md` with gentle dark pine tone, positioned directly above input fields for unambiguous scanning.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Focus:** Muted Sage (`#DDECE3`) background with dark pine typography (`#1E3A2F`).
- **Credentials & Lineage:** Warm Sand Ochre tint (`#F5EEDD`) with deep brass label (`#8F6F26`) displaying accreditation tags (e.g., "M.Sc Yoga", "NIS Certified").

### Specialized Elements
- **Floating Contact Action:** Fixed bottom-right pill containing the WhatsApp channel icon alongside concise booking copy ("Enquire with Master"). Finished in primary pine with high contrast or muted organic botanical green (`#25D366` neutralized to `#2A7352`), offering a tactile thumb target.
- **Timetable / Schedule Block:** Alternating subtle zebra lanes (`#FAF8F5` to `#FFFFFF`) paired with refined vertical dividers to convey class level, teacher, and room allocation without overwhelming the user.
