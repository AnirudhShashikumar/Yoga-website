Pre-Class Preparation

Simple steps 10 mins prior

  • check_circle Light Stomach: Ensure at least 2 hours after a light meal, or practice in the morning on an empty stomach with warm water.
  • check_circle Gentle Props: Keep a yoga mat, a firm cushion or folded blanket for spine elevation, and a water flask nearby.
  • check_circle Camera Placement: If attending online, place camera side-view at waist height so your postures can be safely reviewed and corrected.
  • check_circle Loose Cotton Garments: Wear breathable attire facilitating natural diaphragmatic expansion.
