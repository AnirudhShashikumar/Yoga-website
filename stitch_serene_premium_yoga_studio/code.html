The Guiding Philosophy

Rooted in Authentic Scripture, Grounded in Human Anatomy.

Prabha Yogashala was established to dismantle the misconception that authentic yoga is either rigidly intimidating or diluted into superficial fitness. Guided by a Master of Science in Yoga and formal National Institute of Sports (NIS) accreditation, our shala unifies ancient Hatha wisdom with evidence-led biomechanics.

Whether you are an absolute beginner recovering from postural fatigue, a desk-bound professional seeking nervous system recalibration, or a senior rebuilding joint resilience, every practice is met with precision modifications, compassionate patience, and genuine lineage care.

Frequently Addressed Questions

I've never done yoga before and cannot touch my toes. Can I join? expand_more
Touching your toes is not a prerequisite for yoga—it is merely a natural byproduct of patient, consistent practice. Our classes are structured with individualized variations and yoga props (blocks, straps, bolsters) so that complete beginners and inflexible bodies practice safely without strain.
How do online posture adjustments work effectively? expand_more
We cap virtual batch numbers strictly so that Master Prabha monitors every participant on multi-angle HD screens. You will receive precise verbal cues, alignment checkpoints, and anatomical reminders tailored to what Master observes on your screen.
What equipment or props will I need for my first session? expand_more
For your trial class, a standard non-slip yoga mat and comfortable, breathable stretch attire are all you need. In-studio attendees have full complimentary access to our props. Online students receive advice on simple household alternatives or recommended prop kits after enrollment.
